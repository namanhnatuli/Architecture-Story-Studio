# Research brief: Could We Build an Entire City That Floats?

- Opened: 2026-11-08. Audience: international English-speaking YouTube viewers; the film is in English.
- Format: 10–12 min (acceptable 8–15), 16:9, 24 fps, Studio visual style, illustrative models.

## Central question
Could we design an entire functioning city that floats on water: one that floats, stays in place and keeps working, near a sheltered coast, for about 10,000 people (scenario SCN_FLOATCITY_COASTAL_01)?

Concept stage (2026-11-08): see `concept/CONCEPT_REVIEW.md`. Research questions below are the template's; the floating-city questions are answered in `concept/ENGINEERING_RESEARCH.md`.

## Research questions
1. What physical or functional problem does each form solve?
2. Which materials, climates, sites, costs and skills shaped the choices?
3. Which documented real examples show the decisions (with photographs that have verifiable rights)?
4. Which common misunderstandings must the film avoid?
5. What can the existing asset library show, and what needs a new reference-based asset or a diagram?

## Source policy
- Prefer government agencies, universities, engineering institutions, preservation bodies, primary documents.
- Every claim: verbatim excerpt + locator in `research/sources.json` (SOURCE_VERIFIED). Never read a source you could not open.
- No invented quantities (loads, stresses, capacities, safety factors, rankings). No "linear progress" story, no "one type is best".
- Images: only with a complete rights record (public domain / compatible licence, creator, source URL, original record).
