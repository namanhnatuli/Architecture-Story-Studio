# Why Bridges Don't All Look the Same

Why does one bridge rest on rows of timber posts, another leap the gap in stone curves, and a third become a lattice of iron or steel triangles? This documentary explains how beam, arch and truss bridges carry load, and how materials, sites, spans, budgets and builders shaped the bridges people built.
