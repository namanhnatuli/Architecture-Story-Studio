Why does one bridge rest on rows of timber posts, another leap the gap in stone curves, and another hang from great steel cables? This documentary explains how beam, arch, truss, cantilever, suspension and cable-stayed bridges carry load, and how materials, sites, spans, budgets and builders shaped the bridges people built.
